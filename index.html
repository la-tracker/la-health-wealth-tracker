Why this tracker exists

Your zip code is one of the strongest predictors of your health and financial security. This tool makes that connection visible — so residents, advocates, and policymakers can see where disparities are greatest and act on them.

How the indices work

Each district is scored across three composite indices: financial & community resources (income, school quality, home ownership, nature access), health risk (substance use, chronic disease, life expectancy), and economic policy access (food access, financial services, recreation). Scores range from 0–100.

Built for civic action

This prototype was built by a Los Angeles high school student to inspire youth civic engagement. Data sources include LA County DPH, USC Neighborhood Data for Social Change, and USDA food environment data. Read the methodology →
